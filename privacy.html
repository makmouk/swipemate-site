Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

This policy explains what information SwipeMate collects, how it is used and how you can have it deleted. It covers the SwipeMate mobile app, this website, and the tool we use to publish videos to our own YouTube channel with the YouTube API Services. Questions: mahmoudmakmouk@gmail.com.

1. The SwipeMate app

The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location, and you do not sign in with an account. When you first open it, the app creates:

  • A random install ID: a random code made by the app, used to keep your progress. It is not linked to your identity.
  • A player name: shown on the world ranks. You can change it in the app. Please do not use your real name if you do not want it shown.
  • Puzzle progress: the puzzles you play, whether you solved them, how long you took, hints used, points, streaks and your puzzle rating.

This information is stored on our server to run the game: to choose puzzles at your level, keep your streaks and points, and show the world ranks. Settings such as sound are stored only on your device. We do not sell this information or share it with anyone, except the advertising described below.

2. Advertising

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address and information about the ads you see and tap. This is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (on Android: Settings > Google > Ads).

3. YouTube API Services

SwipeMate uses the YouTube API Services to publish our own short chess puzzle videos to our own YouTube channel. By using any part of SwipeMate that involves YouTube, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

  • Who signs in: only the owner of the SwipeMate YouTube channel, once, to allow our upload tool to publish videos to that channel (permissions: upload videos, and read basic channel information to confirm which channel is connected). App users never sign in with Google and we never access their YouTube accounts.
  • What we access: the channel's ID and name (to confirm the connection), and the ID and privacy status of the videos we upload (to confirm each upload), and the public view, like and comment counts of those videos (to learn which of our videos viewers like best).
  • What we store: the authorization token Google gives us, kept in a file readable only by the upload tool on our server, the IDs and links of the videos we uploaded, in our logs, and the view, like and comment counts of those videos. We do not store any other YouTube data.
  • Sharing: we do not share any YouTube data with anyone.
  • Cookies: the upload tool runs on our server and stores no cookies on anyone's device. This website and the app do not use cookies for YouTube.
  • Revoking access: the channel owner can withdraw our access at any time on the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.
  • Deleting data: when access is withdrawn, or on request by email, we delete the stored token and logs within 7 days. Stored YouTube data is also refreshed or deleted at least every 30 days: our logs and the view counts are kept for 30 days.

4. Videos on social media

We publish our own puzzle videos on YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok. The videos show chess positions from the public-domain Lichess puzzle database and a computer-generated voice. They contain no information about app users.

TikTok: our upload tool uses TikTok's Login Kit and Content Posting API to send our videos to our own TikTok account. Only the account owner signs in, once, granting the permissions to read basic account information (user.info.basic) and to upload videos (video.upload). We store the authorization tokens, readable only by the upload tool on our server, and the IDs of the videos we send. We do not access or store any other TikTok data, and we share nothing. The owner can remove our access at any time in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy > Security > Manage app permissions); we then delete the stored tokens within 7 days.

To learn which of our videos viewers like best, we read the statistics the platforms give us about our own videos (views, likes, comments, shares, saves and average watch time). These are totals for each video; they contain no information about individual viewers.

5. Keeping and deleting your data

App progress is kept while you use the app. To have your progress and player name deleted, email mahmoudmakmouk@gmail.com with your player name; we delete it within 30 days. Uninstalling the app removes the information stored on your device.

6. Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access to our server and to stored tokens is limited to the SwipeMate developer.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
