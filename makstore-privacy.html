MAK STORE

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Mak Store is a toy and sports equipment shop in Lebanon. Customers order through WhatsApp. This policy covers "Mak store", the tool the shop uses to publish posts and run ads on its own Facebook Page through Meta's APIs. Questions: mahmoudmakmouk@hotmail.com.

1. Who uses the tool

Only the shop owner signs in to the tool, with their own Facebook account. Customers and other people never sign in to it, and it does not ask anyone for personal information.

2. What it reads and stores

  • Access tokens that Facebook gives the shop owner, so the tool can publish posts and manage ads for the Mak Store Page.
  • The Page's own posts: their text, links and public counts (views, reactions, comments).
  • Ad results from the shop's ad account: spend, reach, clicks and the number of WhatsApp chats started, including totals by region, age group, gender and placement. Meta reports these as totals that do not identify anyone.

The tool does not read customers' WhatsApp messages, profiles or phone numbers. Chats and orders stay in WhatsApp.

3. How the information is used

Only to publish the shop's posts, choose which posts to advertise and with what budget, and report the results to the shop owner. It is kept on the shop's own computer. The ad results are reviewed with the help of an AI assistant (Claude, by Anthropic). We do not sell or share this information with anyone else.

4. Deleting your data

To remove the tool's access, go to Facebook Settings > Business integrations (or Apps and websites) and remove "Mak store". We then delete the stored tokens within 7 days. To have any other information deleted, email mahmoudmakmouk@hotmail.com: we delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

Customers who ordered through WhatsApp can ask us, on WhatsApp or by email, to delete their order details.

5. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes too.
